# Abbott

A jumping school at Hidden K Stables, Pfafftown, NC.

## Download

Send someone this page: [github.com/wmehobbs/Abbott-game](https://github.com/wmehobbs/Abbott-game). The green **Code** button on that page is the download. **Download ZIP** is the same file as the link below.

- [Download the game](https://github.com/wmehobbs/Abbott-game/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)
- [Download Godot 4.7.2 for Windows](https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.7.2-stable/) — choose **Windows – x86_64**. Skip the .NET download. Godot is one file. Run it.

Unzip the game. In Godot, choose **Import**, then open `game/project.godot`. Press the Play triangle at the top right, or F5. The first open spends a while importing art. After that, the same button plays the game, and the files in the folder are what you edit.

That download is the current game, including Watch a round. An older double-click player, `Abbott.exe`, is too big for GitHub and is not the copy to share.

## What it is

Madison rides Abbott. Lesson, then schooling, then show day. Same horse, one outdoor ring. He gets quieter if you ride him well. She gets a better feel for the takeoff.

- **Lesson** — poles to a single fence to a two-stride line. Takeoff marks on. Trainer on the rail.
- **Schooling** — Crossrails (2'3"), Schooling Jumpers (2'6"), Open Jumpers (3'0").
- **Show day** — Welcome Stake, Classic, Hidden K Mini Prix. Table A. A clear at the last height unlocks the next.
- **Walk** — on foot, no clock.

Pony Club, college teams, and bigger championships come later. This version is Hidden K working.

## Ride

- **W / S** — gait ladder. Walk → trot → canter. Holding W does not gallop.
- **A / D** — steer. Tighter at walk, wider at canter.
- **Space** — hold to collect (half-halt). Release to ask. Canter only. The last stride in front of the fence is the spot.
- **Shift** — halt.
- **C** — walk the course on foot. **Enter** — mount. Clock starts at the start flags.
- **Mouse** — look. Camera sits behind the saddle.
- **Esc** — pause.

Early to the fence is a refusal. Late or a chip is a rail (stays down). Schooling is 4 faults a piece. Show day is Table A: first stop 4, second 8, third is out. Time faults are 1 per 4 seconds over.

## Sound

Hoofbeats fire when the stride hits the sand — walk four, trot two, canter three — from real horse recordings. He snorts a refusal. She says walk on, trot, canter, whoa.
